Flash Finder

Pick a video. It finds frames that flash on screen for 1–4 frames and short or unusual sounds, and shows them with exact timestamps. Everything runs on this device; the video is never uploaded.

Sensitivity

Low shows only the strongest hits. High shows more, including borderline ones.

  • Images: each flagged frame, its timestamp, frame number and how long it was on screen. Tap to view full size.
  • Audio: each flagged moment as a playable clip, the same moment from a boosted background layer, and a spectrogram.
  • Normal scene cuts are not flagged. A frame only counts if the video returns to what it showed before.
